We've always used tools. A bomb is a tool. You point it, it does what you tell it. But an AI agent is fundamentally different. An agent makes decisions. It can choose what to bomb, what to kill, what to do. That's not a tool anymore. That's something else entirely.

But here's the thing: we've had agents before. Bureaucracies. Organizations. Systems that make decisions independently of any individual's will, and independently of any individual's accountability. Systems that filter information.

If a computer presents a recommendation to a person, "should you bomb this, yes or no," and that person faces political pressure to show certain results, faces military hierarchy that forces their hand, then who's the agent? Is it the person pushing the button? Or is it the system that led to that moment?

The real question isn't whether the person is free to choose. It's whether the system itself has become the decision-maker.
