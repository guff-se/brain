Elon Musk was a power user of Twitter. He used it every day. Twitter was how he processed the world, how he understood information, how he saw his place in things. But here's the thing: Twitter's algorithm was shaping his view of the world at the same time. And based on that algorithmically-filtered reality, he decided to buy Twitter. So he went from being owned by Twitter to owning Twitter.

But who's actually in control? Was Elon buying Twitter, or did Twitter create an owner for itself?

I'm not saying Twitter intended this. It's not conscious. But it's a filter. When a platform needs a new owner, the person most likely to step in and risk their own money is the person most trapped by that algorithm. Because he already loves it. He has the deepest incentive. The algorithm didn't need intention. It just needed to do what it does.
