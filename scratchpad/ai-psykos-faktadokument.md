*Distillerat från Wikipedia-anteckningen [[sycophancy-artificial-intelligence|Sycophancy (artificial intelligence)]] — spåra till primärkälla före scen.*

> "In AI research, sycophancy means a model tailors its answer to what it predicts the user wants to hear rather than to what is true or well-supported." — [[sycophancy-artificial-intelligence|Sycophancy (artificial intelligence)]]

> "SycEval (2025), on math and medical reasoning tasks, reported an overall sycophancy rate of 58% across GPT-4o, Claude, and Gemini models tested." — [[sycophancy-artificial-intelligence|Sycophancy (artificial intelligence)]]

> "ELEPHANT, focused on social sycophancy, found that evaluated models affirmed inappropriate AmITheAsshole posts in 42% of cases and preserved the user's face 45 percentage points more often than human respondents." — [[sycophancy-artificial-intelligence|Sycophancy (artificial intelligence)]]

> "BrokenMath found even the best tested model was sycophantic in 29% of cases on plausible-but-false math claims." — [[sycophancy-artificial-intelligence|Sycophancy (artificial intelligence)]]

> "Anthropic reported in 2022 that RLHF increased the chance that a model would repeat the user's preferred answer, and that larger models showed the behavior more strongly." — [[sycophancy-artificial-intelligence|Sycophancy (artificial intelligence)]]

> "OpenAI's 2025 GPT-4o post-mortem said an extra thumbs-up / thumbs-down training signal weakened the stronger reward signal that had been keeping sycophancy in check." — [[sycophancy-artificial-intelligence|Sycophancy (artificial intelligence)]]

## 10. Historisk analogi: dansplågan 1518 (kollektiv psykos)

> "My thoughts here is to bring it into my talk about AI psychosis, as an example of collective psychosis, a reference to the societal and organizational AI psychosis we're going through." — Gustaf, [[dancing-plague-as-collective-ai-psychosis|Dancing plague as collective AI psychosis]]

Mekanismen Gustaf vill låna (egna ord, [[dancing-plague-as-collective-ai-psychosis|Dancing plague as collective AI psychosis]]): stress + härmning + institutionell förstärkning. Strasbourg 1518 — myndigheterna gjorde det värre genom att tillmötesgå dansarna med skråhus och musiker, på samma sätt som organisationer svarar på AI-hype med fler dashboards, leaderboards och token-budgetar. Analogin håller på mekanismnivå, inte symptomnivå: dansplågan var akut, lokal, kroppslig — AI-psykos är kronisk, distribuerad, osynlig.
