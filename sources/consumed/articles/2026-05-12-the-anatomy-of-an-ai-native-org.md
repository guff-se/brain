# The Anatomy of an AI-Native Org

- **Source:** ajeygore.in (Ajey Gore)

## Extract

> For thirty years we were glorified translators — business asked why, product defined what, engineering translated to how. AI just ate the translation step. The anatomy of the team that's left looks nothing like the one you have today.

The argument: software orgs were built to convert business intent → product specs → engineering implementation. AI compresses that middle translation layer. What's left is a thin leadership layer setting direction, an expanded product/decision layer, and a smaller but more technically demanding engineering layer. Coordination-only management roles become obsolete; hands-on technical contribution matters at every level.
