## Extract

> "The brand that feels human in the AI era earns the decade ahead." The report presents striking findings: 74% of consumers believe the internet feels less human than a decade ago, and the average person experiences "bot fatigue" within 40 minutes of online interactions. Most notably, 61% of consumers cannot identify a single brand executing AI messaging effectively.
