# Is the iPhone Birth Control? Causal Evidence from AT&T's 2007-2011 Carrier Monopoly

## Extract

The paper treats the iPhone's 2007-2011 AT&T exclusivity as a natural experiment and argues that smartphone diffusion can explain a large share of the U.S. fertility decline after 2007. The estimated effects are strongest for younger cohorts, and the authors connect the mechanism to reduced in-person interaction, more pornography use, and lower sexual frequency.
