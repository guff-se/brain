# Only 16 percent of Americans think AI will have a positive impact on society, a new study shows

- **Author:** Lucas Ropek

- **Captured:** 2026-06-17

## Extract

> Despite the fact that AI increasingly dominates our economy (it's a hot IPO summer and we're all just along for the ride), most Americans are not particularly optimistic about the technology's long-term impact on the country, a new study from Pew Research reveals. In fact, although a whole lot of Americans increasingly use AI in their daily lives, most of them have neutral to negative views about it, the research reveals. Only 16% of Americans think that AI's impact on society during the next 20 years will be positive, Pew says, while around 40% say that it will have a negative impact.
