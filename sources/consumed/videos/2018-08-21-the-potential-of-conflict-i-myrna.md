Conflict Matters Conference Brussels 2015
Opening lecture by Myrna Lewis, Deep Democracy, South Africa

Conflict Matters is a major European conference to tackle a crucial issue - dealing with conflict in a constructive way, in schools, in families, in society. The 2015 edition opens with a series of keynote speeches that explore the notion of conflict from challenging and sometimes unexpected perspectives. These engaging presentations shed light on the biological origins and social implications of conflict and highlight its potential for learning and transformation, both on personal and societal levels. 

Organized by the Evens Foundation in partnership with the Erasmus Hogeschool, Escolu da Cultura de Pau, Flemish Peace Institute, ATEE, The Children's Rights Commissioner, Georg Eckert Institute, L'Université de Paix, Learning for Well-being Foundation, Foundation Bernheim. 
Brussels, 21-23 October, 2015
