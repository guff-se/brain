Pranksters and Porters, Urchins and Unicorns, Queers and Queens - Gather!
Geeks and Gnomes, Nymphs and Nomads, Yogis and Youngsters - Gather!
Dorks and Dumpsterdivers, Witches and Workers, Clergy and Clowns - Gather!
Through time and space, you hear the ringing of a bell. The sound of unity calling out through the vails of dreams and reality. Calling out for a gathering, in that land, on the border between. A gathering in the honor of gifting.

This is what will happen:

Find your tribe.
At 19.00. each “tribe” (camp, family, neighborhood or group of friends) will take initiative to gather for their own individual opening ceremony. The goal is to create a sense of belonging with those closest to you, to be able to share expectations, hopes and fears. How you do this is completely up to you.
If you feel you have the capacity, please be inclusive. Remember that many participants are not a part of a camp, and might appreciate to be invited to yours.

Prepare your gift.
As a tribe, prepare a gift to share at the Gathering. The gift could come in any shape or form: a treat, a performance, a dance, a game, a secret handshake or a piece of music. Express your tribe’s identity through this gift. Feel free to plan this gift out in advance.

Gather
At 21.00, we all gather around the "Stomach Plaza" at the center of the main city. Stick with your tribe. Wait for the signal. The sharing of gifts will begin at the sound of the horn.

Listen, and co-create
At the sound of the Great Horn of Ages, we will start sharing our gifts. But for each person you meet, you will listen in and get inspired. Bit by bit, you will lose your identity as a tribe and as an individual, to more and more become a part of a group flow. Don't small talk or argue. Listen with your body, listen with your emotions, listen with your ears. If you speak, speak with intention. Be a, “Yes”. Let go of your personal vision, resonate and find flow.

Flow.
Whatever happens, happens. The ritual ends when it ends. Whatever happened was exactly what was supposed to happen.

Welcome to the Opening Ceremony of The Borderland.
