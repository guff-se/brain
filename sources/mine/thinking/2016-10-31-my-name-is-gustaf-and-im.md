My name is Gustaf, and I'm a racist.
This morning, I saw an Arabic-looking man carrying a strange package at the airport and immediately had the thought that it could be a bomb. I knew the thought was irrational, I knew it was wrong, but that couldn't keep the thought from spontaneously appearing.
We are all have racist thoughts, it is something very human to use categorization to try to simplify the world. All we can do is be aware of it and strive to get better.
