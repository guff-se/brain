Let’s rewind this shall we?
Copyright, the idea that you can own knowledge inside another person’s brain, notebook or computer, is an abomination. It’s a violation of human rights. Copying is the foundation of life itself, a virtuous and sacred act. The right to copying shall not be infringed for the purpose of profit.
Also: piracy does not hurt sales (for those who care about money more than rights).
