I've never understood the worship of self-control. Why should we restrain and confine ourselves based on fantasies of hypothetical futures? To me, it seems more practical to be honest and embracing of our own will, to be able to communicate it and be proud of it. Sure, you will not always get what you want, but the fact that you DO want something is nothing to he ashamed of - it should be celebrated!
