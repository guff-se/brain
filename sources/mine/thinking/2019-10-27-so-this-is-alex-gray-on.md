So, this is Alex Gray, on top of Abraxas promoting a album released by Sony Music. Alex did the album artwork, something I must assumed he got paid for (by a Sony Music or a subsidiary). Hundreds of people just cheering, no one seem to react at this blatant act of commercial advertising. 

Should the creative industries exempt from the Decommodification principle?

Also, should we not hold adored community leaders like Alex Gray to a higher standard? This is an individual that many people look up to within the Burning Man community. If this man, who is successful enough to easily be able to live off his art, can come to Burning Man to promote his paid work - then why can't I?
