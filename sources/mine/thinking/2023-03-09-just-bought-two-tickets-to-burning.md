Just bought two tickets to Burning Man at the humble total of... $1,489.48!

But i guess that's what happens with success. There is just no incentive to try to keep your price down when you know you will sell out no matter what. Abundance bread bloated inefficient dinosaurs.

Will I go anyway? Well, I don't know. It's been five year and I miss it. But it just feels wrong. I feel ashamed of nurturing such an unabashedly wasteful beast. It's so far from the values I stand for myself. It's so far from the community I love and nurture here in Sweden.

How will Borderland avoid the same fate?
Maybe not in 5 years, but in 25?
