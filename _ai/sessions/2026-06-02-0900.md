## Key decisions

- Quotes section placed after Implication (reference material, not argument).
- No cap on quote count — if a source has 8 relevant passages, all 8 are included.
- Gustaf's own words attributed to "Gustaf"; external authors by name where identifiable.
- Secondary quotations (e.g., Jung quoted inside Gustaf's notes) noted with a bracketed clarifier.
